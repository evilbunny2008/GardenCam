First Garden Cam release, forked from ONVIF Camera 2.5.0 (itself a fork of Cams).

- New app ID: installs alongside Cams and ONVIF Camera
- Lower PTZ latency: smaller stream cache, no audio on PTZ streams
- "Tune camera for low latency": sets H.264 Baseline and CBR over ONVIF
- ONVIF credentials encrypted with the Android Keystore
- Android 17 support, including local network access
- Fixed motion-event subscriptions not being released
